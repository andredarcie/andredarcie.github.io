Tiny Tribes

Grow your Blue Tribe, raise an army and burn down every Red Tribe building.

  • 👆Tap a unit to select it. Double-tap selects all of that kind on screen.
  • ✊Hold, then drag to lasso a group.
  • 🌳With workers selected, tap a tree or berry bush to gather, or the ground to walk.
  • ⚔️With troops selected, tap an enemy to attack, or the ground to advance.
  • 🛖Tap a building to train units there. Build Houses for more population.
  • 🤏Drag to move the camera, pinch to zoom, tap the minimap to jump.
